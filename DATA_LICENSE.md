# Data licence

**Code** in this repository is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).

**Data we derive** — scores, ranks, pillar and composite values, cleaned and harmonised
tables, and the bulk downloads published by the site — is released under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Please credit "Unnati Index" and link to the site.

**Source data** stays under the terms of its original publisher. Every dataset records its
publisher, licence and required attribution in `pipeline/unnati/registry.yaml` and on the
site's `/sources` page. In particular:

| Source family | Terms |
|---|---|
| data.gov.in (Open Government Data Platform India) | [Government Open Data License – India (GODL)](https://data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india) — attribution required |
| Ministries, NSO/MoSPI, NCRB, RGI, RBI and other government publications | Reproduced with attribution and a link to the original publication |
| PhonePe Pulse | [CDLA-Permissive-2.0](https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/) |
| Wikidata (office-holders) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Map boundaries | Survey of India official boundaries (via DataMeet); see `geo/README.md` |

If you believe any data here is used outside its terms, please open an issue and we will fix it.
